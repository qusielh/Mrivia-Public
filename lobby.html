🌸 COMMUNITY GUIDELINES & SAFETY NOTICES 🌸

Rules & Safety Guidelines

Please review these notes to keep your experience safe, fun, and secure:

🌸 Official Website & Anti-Impersonation Notice

This is the only official Mrivia website created solely by Creator. Creator does not have public social media accounts, alternative community pages, or external payment links anywhere else on the internet.

Never trust anyone claiming to represent Creator or soliciting funds outside of this website. If any official links or campaigns are ever launched, they will be announced right here! You can always reach out directly using the Feedback or Q&A button.

🛡️ Chat Safety & Reporting

Never share sensitive personal info in the chat. If you choose to share your Discord tag or social handle, that responsibility is entirely on you.

Never listen to anyone attempting to lie, manipulate, or exploit you. Always report any troll, scammer, or harmful behavior by taking a clean screenshot that clearly shows both their username and the chat!

⚠️ IF YOU ARE A KID, PLEASE READ THIS:

I know you just want to play games and have fun, but please do not share any personal info whatsoever. You are completely protected from anything bad people might tell you.

Do not answer personal questions strangers ask you, and don't let emotional tricks fool you. Your safety matters above everything else—you can always report them and I will ban them as soon as I can.

⛏️ Gameplay Optimization Notes

Word pools have been intentionally optimized so you don't need to stress over material variations. You don't guess "DIAMOND PICKAXE"—simply guess "PICKAXE"! The same applies to SWORD, DOOR, STAIRS, SPEAR, etc... Pixelary allows you to use custom colors and save them in the bottom slots, if you spend a whole round/attempt without submitting anything you will be disconnected (afk), and you can come back anytime by submitting an answer.

If you ever spot a missing mob or item, please let me know! I will gladly update them. Feel free to leave feedback—my ultimate goal is to make sure everyone has fun.

📱 Mobile Players: Permanent Fullscreen & App Install

Want to play without the browser address bar getting in your way? You can install Mrivia as a native-feeling app!

How to install: Tap your mobile browser's menu (⋮ three dots on Chrome or Share on Safari) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". Once installed, launching Mrivia from your home screen keeps the game in permanent horizontal fullscreen across all matches with zero browser clutter!

Troubleshooting: If your browser doesn't show "Install app" immediately, tap "Add shortcut" or "Add to Home screen" first, or refresh the page and check the menu again.
